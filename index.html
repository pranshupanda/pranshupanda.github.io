Research · University of Minnesota

Choosing the next experiment when every experiment is expensive.

In protein design, each candidate means a lab experiment, so the goal is a good design in as few experiments as possible. Bayesian optimization learns from past results to choose what to test next. Choosing well is hard when the options are discrete sequences, where gradient methods don’t apply, so I compared evolutionary search algorithms for that step. I built GPU-accelerated PyTorch pipelines and tested them on LABS, a standard hard benchmark.
